# Document workbench — Spec

The workbench's domain is the company's governed knowledge, and that is defined by the corpus, not here: see the [corpus spec](../corpus/spec.md). The workbench reads it only through the published package.

## Requirements

Not yet written as requirements. Until they are, what the first slice must do, and how it is checked, is in [pco-design.md](pco-design.md): the PCO definition, the pricing engine, structured drafting, Word rendering and the acceptance cases.

## Boundaries

- **Not in the domain:** the company's knowledge itself. Rules, values, clauses and templates are read from the package; none are held in the workbench repository.
- **Not in the domain:** project records, which stay in Procore or Buildertrend.
