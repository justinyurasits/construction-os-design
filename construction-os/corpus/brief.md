# Corpus — Brief

Only what narrows or adds to the [Product Brief](../brief.md) for the corpus. The customer, the painful moment and the wedge are there.

## Who relies on it, and when

- **The Policy Owner** maintains it: changes a rule value, approves a document change, answers the onboarding questions that set the company's values.
- **Every Construction OS part that needs the company's knowledge** reads it, through the published package: the document workbench when it drafts and prices, and the advisor when it answers how the company does something.
- **PMs and superintendents** never open it. They rely on it every time a draft cites a rule or applies a rate.

## What it must make possible

- Every rule, value and clause the company governs has one authoritative source, with an ID a draft can cite.
- A company setting changes in one place, and every output and check that depends on it follows, with no core document edited.
- A second company can be onboarded from the same starter library without editing the first company's documents.
- Its correctness can be checked on its own, apart from retrieval or drafting.

## Out of scope

- The applications that use it. The workbench and any later part read the package; none of them owns it.
- Project context: anything supplied for one project and the drafts made from it.
- Project records, which stay in Procore or Buildertrend.
