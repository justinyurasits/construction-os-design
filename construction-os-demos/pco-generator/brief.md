# PCO Generator — Brief

**Draft**, taken from the code repo's README.

## The painful moment

A change order decides whether work performed gets paid for. It's written under time
pressure by a project manager on a job site, so change work routinely gets done before
anything is authorized in writing, and that margin is the most commonly eaten margin in
the business. The slow part isn't the decision; it's producing the document.

## What it does

A project manager describes what changed. The tool returns a complete change order:
specific scope language, pricing looked up from a cost database (never generated by the
model), and the structure of an established change order standard, as Word and PDF.
